# Community Language Barrier & Speech Technology Survey (Telugu–English)
### Project: PAASR (Phonology-Aware Adaptive Speech Representation for Telugu-to-English S2ST)

---

## 1. Information & Ethical Consent Statement
- **Purpose of Study:** This research investigates spoken language barriers experienced by Telugu native speakers when communicating with English-speaking professionals (doctors, educators, administrative officials, and financial officers). The goal is to design an AI-powered, voice-to-voice translation system that does not require typing or reading English.
- **Data Privacy & Governance:** This survey is strictly **non-identifying**. No names, government IDs, phone numbers, or private audio recordings are collected without explicit written institutional consent.
- **Voluntary Participation:** Participation is voluntary. You may skip any question or stop at any time.
